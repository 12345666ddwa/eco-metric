04 / The apple

What a rested teacher passes on.

Marking is not the point of teaching. It is the thing that eats the time teaching needs.

The 5.7 hours a week spent marking comes to roughly 228 hours a year across a 40 week school year. That is five and a half full working weeks, spent away from lesson design, feedback conversations, and the students in front of the class.

Give those hours back and the classroom changes.

Computed from 5.7 hours per week across a 40 week school year. Arithmetic, not a prediction.

Source: TALIS 2024 weekly marking hours. Annual figure is our own arithmetic on a stated 40 week assumption.

05 / The pen

For every five minutes a student writes, the teacher spends twenty.

One teacher described the arithmetic of a single question: five minutes for a student to answer, ten to read it properly, ten more to write feedback that means anything.

Sourced from the team interview with an NSW secondary school Enterprise Computing teacher.

06 / The word

Amateur. From amare. To love.

Nobody enters teaching for the marking. They enter it because they love the subject, and they want to hand it on.

Sixty per cent of teachers name marking as the single most stressful part of the job. Not the students, not the content. The paperwork that surrounds it.

60%
say marking is the most stressful part of the role.

Hover for values.

Source: YouGov survey on teacher workload, cited in the team research document. Etymology: Latin amator, one who loves.

07 / What changes

A rested teacher's shelter and passion radiates to all.

The lever is not more hours. It is fewer hours spent on work that does not need a human, so the hours that do need a human are possible again.

Every hour returned to lesson design is an hour spent on the part of teaching that made the profession worth entering. The data says the resource exists. It is inside the marking load.

If marking time dropped by half

114 h
returned per teacher per year, to lesson design or to leaving work at work. Scenario arithmetic on the 228 hour baseline, not a forecast.

That is the whole story, in one number.

Source: Arithmetic on TALIS 2024 marking hours. Scenario, labelled as such.

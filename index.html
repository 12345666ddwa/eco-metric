04

What a rested teacher passes on

Marking is not the point of teaching. It is the thing that eats the time teaching needs.

5.7 hours a week comes to roughly 228 hours a year across a 40 week school year. That is five and a half full working weeks spent away from lesson design, from feedback conversations, and from the students in front of the class.

give those hours back and the room changes

Computed from 5.7 hours a week over a 40 week year. Arithmetic, not a prediction.

Source TALIS 2024 weekly marking hours. The annual figure is our own arithmetic on a stated 40 week assumption.

05

For five minutes of writing, twenty minutes of marking

One teacher described the arithmetic of a single question: five minutes for a student to answer, ten to read it properly, ten more to write feedback that means anything.

  • Student answering5 min
  • Teacher reading10 min
  • Teacher writing feedback10 min

Per question, per class. Multiply by every class, every term.

Source Team interview, NSW secondary school Enterprise Computing teacher.

06

Amateur. From amare. To love.

Nobody enters teaching for the marking. They enter because they love the subject and want to hand it on.

Sixty per cent name marking as the single most stressful part of the job. Not the students, not the content: the paperwork that surrounds it.

the word itself is the argument

Hover for the value.

Source YouGov survey on teacher workload, cited in the team research document. Etymology: Latin amator, one who loves.

07

A rested teacher's shelter and passion radiates to all

The lever is not more hours. It is fewer hours on work that does not need a human, so the hours that do need a human become possible again.

Every hour returned to lesson design is an hour spent on the part of teaching that made the profession worth entering. The resource already exists. It is inside the marking load.

114h

returned per teacher per year if marking time halved. Scenario arithmetic on the 228 hour baseline, not a forecast.

Source Arithmetic on TALIS 2024 marking hours. Scenario, labelled as such.

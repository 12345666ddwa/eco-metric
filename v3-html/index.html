Quality education starts before the classroom does

"True education does not cloy the mind with facts; it empowers the individual to reduce inequalities, drive gender equality, and foster tolerant societies."

Albert Einstein, attributed. Adopted by the United Nations on education.

Switch views to move through time.

42%

less likely to graduate if the student is food insecure.

Hunger and learning are the same problem: a student who is not fed cannot stay in the room long enough to finish.

Source Public Health Nutrition, 2022 cohort study on food insecurity and degree completion.

03

Teacher burnout is breaking our education system

46.8%

of teachers are considering leaving in the next 12 months, up from 14% in 2021.

52%

report moderate to severe symptoms of depression. General population baseline is 12%.

70%

say their workload is unmanageable.

46.5h

worked per week by a full-time Australian teacher, against an OECD average of 41 hours.

Source TALIS 2024, OECD country note for Australia, and Black Dog Institute teacher mental health data.

04

Appearances can be deceiving

When teachers burn out, students notice. When teachers thrive, students notice that too.

Feedback gets thinner. Lessons get reused. The room still runs, so the system reads it as fine. The children in it read it differently.

walks into the room

Source Team interviews with NSW teachers and students, 2026.

06

A rested teacher's shelter and passion radiates to all

The lever is not more hours. It is fewer hours on work that does not need a human, so the hours that do need a human become possible again.

Marking runs at 5.7 hours a week, roughly 228 hours a year. Halve it and 114 hours come back to lesson design, to feedback conversations, or simply to leaving work at work.

the resource already exists, inside the marking load

Arithmetic on the TALIS 2024 weekly figure across a 40 week year. A scenario, not a forecast.

Source TALIS 2024 weekly marking hours. Annual figure and the halving scenario are our own arithmetic on a stated 40 week assumption.
